photobaer: free online photo editor and Photoshop alternative

photobaer is a free, open-source image editor that runs in your browser. It works offline, keeps your files on your device and opens and saves PSD files. No account, no upload, no install.

Features

  • Layers, groups, layer masks, clipping masks and blend modes
  • Adjustment layers (Levels, Curves, Vibrance and more) and fill layers
  • Layer styles: drop shadow, glow, bevel, stroke, satin and overlays
  • Smart objects and smart filters
  • Selection tools: marquee, lasso, magic wand, quick selection, color range, quick mask
  • Brushes with ABR import, eraser, gradient, paint bucket
  • Retouching: clone stamp, healing brush, spot healing, patch, content-aware fill, dodge, burn
  • Free transform, warp, crop and perspective crop
  • Type, shapes, pen tool, paths and vector masks
  • Open PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF and PSD; save PSD; export PNG, JPEG and WebP
