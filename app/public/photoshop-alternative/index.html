photobaer Open the editor

A free Photoshop alternative that runs in your browser

photobaer is a free, open-source online photo editor for people who know Photoshop. It has layers, adjustment layers, layer styles, smart objects, brushes, retouching tools and PSD open and save. It runs in the browser, works offline and keeps your files on your device.

Start editing, free

Why people switch from Photoshop

  • Free, no subscription. No account, no ads, no trial.
  • Nothing to install. Open photobaer.com in your browser. You can install it as an app if you want.
  • Private. Images are not uploaded. Editing happens on your computer.
  • Works offline. After the first visit you can edit without an internet connection.
  • Your PSD files still work. Open a PSD, edit it and save it as PSD again.
  • Open source. The code is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Tools you already know

photobaer follows the menus, panels and shortcuts that Photoshop users expect, for example Ctrl+T for Free Transform, Ctrl+J to duplicate a layer, Ctrl+U for Hue/Saturation and B for the brush.

  • Layers, groups, layer masks, clipping masks and all common blend modes
  • Adjustment layers such as Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation, Vibrance and Color Lookup
  • Layer styles: drop shadow, inner shadow, outer and inner glow, bevel and emboss, satin, color, gradient and pattern overlay, stroke
  • Smart objects, smart filters and layer comps
  • Selections: marquee, lasso, polygonal and magnetic lasso, magic wand, quick selection, color range, quick mask
  • Painting: brush, pencil, eraser, gradient, paint bucket, ABR brush import
  • Retouching: clone stamp, healing brush, spot healing brush, patch, content-aware move and fill, red eye, dodge, burn, sponge, blur, sharpen, smudge, history brush
  • Transform: free transform, warp, crop, perspective crop, canvas rotation
  • Type and vectors: point, paragraph and on-path text, shapes, pen tool, paths, vector masks, artboards
  • Filters: Gaussian, lens, motion and radial blur, unsharp mask, smart sharpen, add and reduce noise, distort filters, high pass

Other free Photoshop alternatives

If photobaer is not the right fit, these are common choices. Photopea also runs in the browser and opens PSD files; it is free with ads and its source code is closed. GIMP and Krita are open-source desktop apps that you install. Affinity is a desktop app. photobaer is the option for people who want an open-source editor in the browser that works offline and does not upload their images.

Questions

Is photobaer free?

Yes. photobaer is free to use, has no ads and needs no account. The source code is open under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Can photobaer open Photoshop PSD files?

Yes. photobaer opens and saves PSD files with layers, groups, masks, adjustment layers, fill layers, layer styles, smart objects, text layers and shapes kept editable. More about PSD support.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. photobaer edits images in your browser. Files and autosaves stay on your device.

Does photobaer work offline?

Yes. After the first visit photobaer works without an internet connection, and you can install it as an app from the browser.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open photobaer.com in a current desktop browser and start editing.
