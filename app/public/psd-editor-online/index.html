photobaer Open a PSD

Edit PSD files online, free

photobaer opens Photoshop PSD files in your browser and saves them as PSD again. Layers stay layers: masks, adjustment layers, layer styles, smart objects and text remain editable. The file is not uploaded; it stays on your device.

Open a PSD file

How to edit a PSD file online

  1. Open photobaer.com in your browser.
  2. Click Open image or drop the PSD file on the window.
  3. Edit layers, masks, adjustment layers, layer styles or text.
  4. Choose File > Save as PSD, or export PNG, JPEG or WebP.

What photobaer keeps from a PSD

  • Pixel layers, groups, opacity, fill, blend modes and clipping
  • Layer masks and vector masks
  • Adjustment layers, for example Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation and Color Lookup
  • Fill layers: solid color, gradient and pattern
  • Layer styles and blending options, including Blend If
  • Smart objects with their embedded source
  • Text layers as editable type
  • Shape layers and saved paths
  • Layer comps, guides, grid, resolution and artboards

When a document uses a setting that the PSD format or photobaer cannot write, photobaer shows a warning before it saves.

Other formats

photobaer opens PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF and PSD. It saves PSD and its own .pbaer project format, and exports PNG, JPEG and WebP.

Why edit PSD files in photobaer

  • Free, no account and no ads
  • No upload: the PSD is read and written in your browser
  • Works offline after the first visit
  • Open source under the AGPL-3.0 (GitHub)
