photobaer features

photobaer is a free, open-source online photo editor. Everything below runs in your browser, works offline and keeps your files on your device.

Layers

  • Pixel layers, groups, opacity and fill, blend modes, clipping masks
  • Layer masks and vector masks, lock options, multi-select
  • Adjustment layers: Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation, Vibrance, Color Balance, Color Lookup and more
  • Fill layers: solid color, gradient and pattern
  • Smart objects (embedded and linked), smart filters with masks, layer comps

Layer styles

  • Drop shadow, inner shadow, outer glow, inner glow, bevel and emboss, satin
  • Color, gradient and pattern overlay, stroke
  • Blending options with Blend If and knockout, global light, saved styles

Selections

  • Rectangular and elliptical marquee, lasso, polygonal lasso, magnetic lasso
  • Magic wand, quick selection, color range, quick mask
  • Select menu: modify, grow, similar, feather, save and load selection

Painting and brushes

  • Brush, pencil and eraser with pen pressure and tilt
  • Brush Settings: shape dynamics, scattering, texture, dual brush, color dynamics, transfer
  • ABR brush import, brush presets
  • Gradient tool and gradient editor, paint bucket, Fill and Stroke

Retouching

  • Clone stamp and pattern stamp with a Clone Source panel
  • Healing brush, spot healing brush, patch, content-aware move, red eye
  • Content-aware fill
  • Dodge, burn, sponge, blur, sharpen, smudge
  • History brush and art history brush

Transform and canvas

  • Move tool with snapping and smart guides
  • Free transform: scale, rotate, skew, distort, perspective; warp with presets
  • Crop and perspective crop, trim, reveal all, canvas rotation and flips
  • Rulers, guides, grid and artboards

Type and vectors

  • Point, paragraph and on-path text, vertical text, OpenType features, warp text
  • Character and paragraph panels and styles, system fonts in Chromium browsers, font upload
  • Rectangle, ellipse, triangle, polygon, line and custom shapes
  • Pen, freeform and curvature pen, paths panel, path operations

Filters and adjustments

  • Blur: Gaussian, box, lens, motion, radial, shape, smart and surface blur
  • Sharpen: unsharp mask, smart sharpen
  • Noise: add noise, reduce noise, despeckle, dust and scratches, median
  • Distort: displace, pinch, polar coordinates, ripple, shear, spherize, twirl, wave, zigzag
  • Other: high pass, maximum, minimum, offset, custom kernel
  • Image adjustments such as Shadows/Highlights, HDR Toning, Match Color and Replace Color

Files

  • Open PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF and PSD
  • Save PSD; export PNG, JPEG and WebP
  • Autosave in the browser, works offline, installable as an app
